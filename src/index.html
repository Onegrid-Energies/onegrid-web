Our Founder's Story

Born From Darkness,
Built For Light

Growing up in Nigeria, smoke filled my small room every evening, burning my eyes and lungs, but poverty gave me no other option. I still remember trying to study under that weak yellow flame, praying the kerosene would not finish before I finished my homework.

One night while studying with a candle, I mistakenly slept off and fire destroyed my property. Earlier in my life, I had also narrowly survived a devastating flood caused by plastic waste blocking drainage systems in my community. But the experience that truly broke me happened years later.

I watched a local midwife trying to deliver a baby using only candlelight because there was no electricity. The room was dark, hot, and filled with fear. Every second felt dangerous. I could not stop thinking about how something as basic as light could decide whether a mother or child lived or died.

What if waste itself could become hope?

That question gave birth to OneGrid Energies. Today, our work impacts thousands of people every month. Children can now study safely at night. Women no longer inhale kerosene smoke while caring for their families. Rural healthcare centers have better lighting for childbirth and emergencies.

There were nights I slept with tears in my eyes, overwhelmed by the scale of the problems around me. Yet every morning, I woke up with the same determination: to reduce darkness in every form — physical darkness, environmental darkness, and even the hopelessness many young people silently carry inside them.

I believe innovation is not just about technology. It is about humanity.

Even if we cannot solve every problem in the world, while we are here, we can reduce darkness. That is why I continue to build.

Valentine Nnamani

CEO, OneGrid Energies

Our Belief

OneGrid Energies was founded on a simple belief: sustainable change does not always begin with massive systems, but with simple ideas capable of transforming lives.

What started as a vision to reduce energy poverty and environmental waste has grown into a movement focused on reducing darkness across underserved communities.

Through the recycling of waste plastic bottles and discarded laptop batteries, we create solar-powered lanterns that provide light, hope, and opportunity to children and families without reliable access to electricity.

Beyond our community impact projects, we also provide solar installation services, CCTV solutions, renewable energy training, and clean energy products that help homes, businesses, schools, and healthcare centers transition toward sustainable energy.

At OneGrid Energies, we are not just building energy systems. We are building hope, opportunity, and a brighter future.

OnePlastic Initiative

Our Flagship Initiative

Waste becomes light, opportunity, and hope.

Our flagship initiative, OnePlastic, transforms discarded plastic bottles and e-waste lithium-ion batteries from old laptops into affordable solar lanterns designed for low-income and rural households. Instead of allowing these materials to pollute the environment, we convert them into tools for education, safety, and opportunity.

Community Ownership

Power that creates local opportunity.

The lanterns are charged through community-based IoT-enabled solar charging stations strategically installed in underserved communities and managed primarily by rural women. This approach creates local ownership while also generating income opportunities for women who operate the stations.

Business Model

Access first. Sustainable revenue always.

Our business revenue model combines product sales with subscription-based services. This hybrid approach allows us to offer our lanterns at a subsidized rate or no cost and generate recurring income through charging fees.

  • Lanterns are designed for exclusive charging at our stations.
  • Additional revenue streams come from charging mobile phones and other devices.
  • Cloud-based platforms monitor performance, payments, and usage.
Innovation

Designed for people, planet, and progress.

  1. Pay-As-You-Go Model: Customers pay for electricity as they use it, making energy access affordable and flexible.
  2. Smart Solar Charging Stations: IoT-enabled stations provide efficient energy distribution, real-time monitoring, and automated payment collection.
  3. Rechargeable Lanterns: Rechargeable lanterns reduce electronic waste, minimize environmental harm, and provide sustainable lighting.
  4. Women Empowerment: Recruiting and training local women as charging station managers promotes economic empowerment, financial inclusion, and social mobility.
Impact on Local Context

A ripple effect beyond light.

  1. Energy Access: Reliable, affordable, clean energy improves quality of life in off-grid communities.
  2. Economic Empowerment: Jobs and entrepreneurial opportunities for local women stimulate local economic growth and reduce poverty.
  3. Environmental Conservation: Solar power reduces reliance on fossil fuels, mitigates climate change, and promotes sustainable development.
  4. Social Impact: Better education, health, and safety outcomes contribute to a more equitable and just society.
  5. Scalability and Replicability: The model can be replicated in other off-grid communities, creating a ripple effect of positive change.
Scalability

Built to grow where the need is greatest.

Scalability: OneGrid Energies' business model is highly scalable. By leveraging strategic partnerships and collaborations, we can quickly expand our reach to new markets and regions. Our smart solar charging stations and rechargeable lanterns can be easily replicated and distributed to meet growing demand.

Replicability: Our business model is replicable in various regions, particularly in off-grid communities with limited access to electricity. By using locally sourced materials and partnering with local organizations, we can adapt our model to suit different markets and environments.

Cost Structure: By utilizing waste plastic bottles and repurposing lithium-ion batteries, we minimize production costs. Our smart solar charging stations and rechargeable lanterns are designed for efficiency and durability, reducing maintenance and replacement costs.

Revenue Streams

Diversified to sustain the mission.

Our revenue streams are diversified, including:

  1. Charging fees: Customers pay for electricity to charge their lanterns and mobile devices.
  2. Sales of rechargeable lanterns: We generate revenue from the sale of our innovative, eco-friendly lanterns.
  3. Partnerships and collaborations: Strategic partnerships with organizations and companies provide additional revenue streams.
Growth Potential

Ready for the next horizon.

OneGrid Energies has significant growth potential, driven by:

  1. Increasing demand for off-grid energy solutions: Demand for reliable, affordable, and clean energy is rising, particularly in Africa.
  2. Expanding partnerships and collaborations: Strategic partnerships open new markets, technologies, and funding opportunities.
  3. Continued innovation and R&D: Our commitment to innovation helps us stay ahead of the competition and capitalize on emerging opportunities.
Technical Feasibility

Built on proven, practical technology.

Technology

  1. Smart Solar Charging Stations: Advanced IoT enables real-time monitoring, automated payment collection, and efficient energy distribution.
  2. Rechargeable Lanterns: Cutting-edge LED technology provides high-efficiency lighting while minimizing energy consumption.
  3. Lithium-Ion Battery Repurposing: Our proprietary process repurposes batteries from discarded laptops, reducing e-waste and environmental impact.

Components

  1. Solar Panels: High-efficiency panels provide a reliable source of renewable energy.
  2. Battery Management Systems: Advanced BMS ensure efficient charging, discharging, and battery-state monitoring.
  3. IoT Modules: Modules facilitate real-time monitoring, automation, and data analytics.
Manufacturing Aspects

Made locally, made to last.

  1. Local Production: We employ women and youths to produce rechargeable lanterns, reducing transportation costs and supporting local economies.
  2. Modular Design: Smart solar charging stations and rechargeable lanterns use modular components for easy maintenance, repair, and upgrading.
  3. Quality Control: Rigorous quality-control measures ensure high standards of performance, reliability, and safety.
