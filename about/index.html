From a single spark of conviction in Onitsha to 12,000 lives touched across Nigeria — this is the story of OneGrid Energies.

Our Belief

OneGrid Energies was founded on a simple belief: sustainable change does not always begin with massive systems, but with simple ideas capable of transforming lives.

What started as a vision to reduce energy poverty and environmental waste has grown into a movement focused on reducing darkness across underserved communities.

Through the recycling of waste plastic bottles and discarded laptop batteries, we create solar-powered lanterns that provide light, hope, and opportunity to children and families without reliable access to electricity.

Beyond our community impact projects, we also provide solar installation services, CCTV solutions, renewable energy training, and clean energy products that help homes, businesses, schools, and healthcare centers transition toward sustainable energy.

At OneGrid Energies, we are not just building energy systems. We are building hope, opportunity, and a brighter future.

Our Journey

From Conviction To Movement

2019
The Spark
OneGrid Energies was registered with a mission to reduce energy poverty in Nigeria through innovation and recycling.
2020
OnePlastic Is Born
First prototype lanterns built from waste plastic and discarded laptop batteries. The world's attention begins to turn.
2021
OneHealth Launch
Partnership with rural maternity clinics. Solar boxes delivered to help midwives work safely after sunset.
2022
Community Scale
OneBox stations deployed. Training Academy launched. 40+ green jobs created for young Nigerians.
2024+
National Expansion
12,000+ lives impacted. Investors, NGOs and government agencies take notice. The movement grows.

Sustainability

Built To Last,
Built To Scale

OneGrid Energies isn't built on charity — it's built on circular economics. Every lantern sold funds the next one. Every charging station generates revenue that sustains the community that hosts it.

Our model proves that social impact and commercial viability are not in tension — they amplify each other. We're a social enterprise designed to outlast its funding rounds.

By diverting waste plastics and batteries from landfills, we address two environmental crises simultaneously: solid waste pollution and energy poverty. One problem, one solution, twice the impact.
