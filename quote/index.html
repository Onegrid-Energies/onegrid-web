FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does a solar system cost?

System costs depend on your energy needs, equipment specification, and site requirements. The plans above are starting points; your final quote follows an assessment.

Can solar replace my generator completely?

With the right system size and battery storage, solar can cover the power needs identified during your assessment.

What happens in rainy season?

Solar systems use battery storage and still generate from diffused daylight. We recommend capacity that supports reliable use year-round.

How long does installation take?

Timing depends on system size and site readiness. Our team confirms the installation schedule with your quote.

Do you offer payment options?

Ask the OneGrid team about available payment and financing options for your selected system.
